Fish & chips and kebabs Rothwell, Northamptonshire

Rothwell Town Friar

The shop's menu lived on printed boards and photos shared on social media. Customers had to zoom into images to find a price, and the opening hours were hard to find.

We turned the whole menu into a proper web page that's quick to scan on a phone, grouped by section with jump links. Opening hours, a map and a tap-to-call button sit right at the top, and the design matches the shop's own navy logo.
