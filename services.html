01

Website design

Most of your customers will see your website on a phone, often while they're deciding where to go right now. We design for that moment: what you sell, what it costs, when you're open and how to reach you, all within a thumb's reach.

What's included

  • A design built around your logo and colours
  • Works properly on phones, tablets and desktops
  • Menus, price lists or service pages that you can read without zooming
  • Tap-to-call, map and contact form
  • Basic search set-up so Google understands your pages
  • A walkthrough so you know what you've got

Good for: takeaways, cafés, trades, salons, shops and anyone whose current site is out of date or missing.

02

Web hosting & care

A website needs somewhere to live and someone keeping an eye on it. We host the sites we build, and we can move an existing site over to us too. When your prices or opening hours change, send us a message and we'll update it.

What's included

  • Hosting with a secure (https) certificate
  • Regular backups, so nothing is lost
  • Software and security updates handled for you
  • Small content changes, like prices, hours and photos
  • Email addresses at your own domain, on request
  • Month to month, no long contract

Moving from another host? We'll handle the transfer and keep your site online while we do it.

03

Google & local search

When someone types "chippy near me" or "plumber in Kettering", Google shows a map with three businesses. Getting into those three is mostly about having an accurate, complete and active Google Business Profile. That's what we sort out.

What's included

  • Set up or tidy up your Google Business Profile
  • Correct hours, photos, categories and services
  • Make sure your name, address and phone match everywhere
  • Help collecting and replying to reviews
  • A monthly note on calls, direction requests and views
04

Social media & ads

Regular posts keep regulars coming back, and a small, well-aimed ad can reach people within a few miles of your door. You decide the budget, and you can change or pause it whenever you like.

What's included

  • Facebook and Instagram page set-up and tidy-up
  • Posts planned and written for you
  • Local ads targeted by area and interest
  • Your ad spend paid directly to Meta, so you see every penny
  • Simple monthly results: who saw it, who clicked, what it cost

How billing works: a 30-day rolling contract paid by Direct Debit through GoCardless. Your adverts start within 48 hours of your first payment. Full terms.

Questions

Things people usually ask.

Anything else? Send us a message.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on how many pages you need and what they have to do. A one-page site for a local shop costs a lot less than an online store. After a short chat we'll send a fixed written quote, and that's the price you pay.

Will I own my website and domain?

Yes. Your domain is registered in your name and the site is yours. If you decide to move to another company, we'll hand over everything you need.

Can I update the site myself?

If you'd like to, we can set that up and show you how. Most clients prefer to send us a quick message and let us do it, which is included with hosting for small changes.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. Advertising is a 30-day rolling contract with no long-term commitment. You can cancel any time by giving 7 days' written notice before your next billing date. There are no refunds for part months. Read the full terms.

How do I pay?

By monthly Direct Debit, set up through GoCardless. Payments are taken every 30 days from the date of your first payment, and your adverts start within 48 hours of that first payment.

I already have a website. Can you help?

Yes. We can look at what you've got and tell you honestly whether it needs a rebuild, a few fixes, or just better hosting.
