Our promises

Set up for your benefit, not ours.

A lot of small businesses have been burned by long contracts, hidden fees and websites they can't edit. Here's how we work instead.

  1. You own it all

    Your domain, your website files, your Google and social accounts are registered in your name. If you ever leave, you take everything with you.

  2. A fixed price before we start

    You get a written quote that says exactly what's included. If something extra comes up, we ask first. No surprise invoices.

  3. No long contracts

    Advertising runs on a 30-day rolling contract. Cancel any time with 7 days' written notice before your next billing date. See terms.

  4. Reports in plain English

    Each month you hear how many people found you, called you or asked for directions, and what we're doing next. No jargon.
