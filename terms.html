Service summary

You are signing up for a 30-day rolling advertising contract with Britz Advertiser, powered by Laser Marketing Ltd. This service is billed monthly by Direct Debit through GoCardless.

    All payments are collected by GoCardless Direct Debit.

    Payments are taken every 30 days from the date of the first payment. This is a rolling monthly contract with no long-term commitment.

    You may cancel at any time with 7 days' written notice before the next billing date. No refunds are issued for partial months.

    If a Direct Debit payment fails, we reserve the right to pause advertising services until payment is received.
